engineering judgement.

This is the **personal scope**: portable, and it leaves with George.
Never Thanx-specific: no internal systems, customers, people, incidents, code
or metrics. When unsure, it belongs in the thanx scope instead.

## Rules

- Entries are added, edited and retired only through `/reflect`, with George's

- Every entry carries an `as-of` date.
- Entries George marks wrong are deleted, not annotated.
